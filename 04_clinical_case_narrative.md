ICSR Clinical Case Safety Narrative
Safety Report Unique Identifier: AER-2026-IND-00101
Suspect Product: Jardiance (Empagliflozin 10 mg Oral OD)
Adverse Event: Euglycemic Diabetic Ketoacidosis (EDKA)
Case Classification: Serious (Hospitalization) | Spontaneous Initial Report
CLINICAL NARRATIVE:
This initial spontaneous report was received on 12-OCT-2026 from an endocrinologist in India concerning a 54-year-old female patient (weight: 68 kg) with a 6-year documented history of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and dyslipidemia.
Concomitant medication included Metformin Hydrochloride 500 mg orally twice daily.
On 15-AUG-2026, the patient initiated therapy with Empagliflozin (Jardiance) 10 mg orally once daily for glycemic control.
On 09-OCT-2026 (approximately 55 days post therapy initiation), the patient presented to the emergency department with severe malaise, nausea, tachypnea, and abdominal discomfort. Arterial blood gas analysis confirmed high anion gap metabolic ketoacidosis (pH 7.18, serum bicarbonate 11 mEq/L, elevated beta-hydroxybutyrate levels) accompanied by normal blood glucose levels (168 mg/dL), clinically consistent with Euglycemic Diabetic Ketoacidosis (EDKA).
The patient was admitted as an inpatient (hospitalized) for stabilization, intravenous fluid resuscitation, and an insulin-dextrose infusion protocol. Empagliflozin therapy was permanently discontinued on 10-OCT-2026. Following drug withdrawal and standard clinical management, the metabolic acidosis resolved, and the patient was discharged in recovered condition on 14-OCT-2026. Rechallenge was not attempted.
CAUSALITY & REGULATORY ASSESSMENT:
Given the plausible temporal sequence, known SGLT2-inhibitor pharmacological class effect, absence of alternative precipitating factors, and documented recovery upon dechallenge, the causal relationship between Empagliflozin and the reported euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis is considered Probable.
The event meets the ICH E2A seriousness criterion of Inpatient Hospitalization. Because this is a serious spontaneous post-marketing safety report, it is assigned a 15-Day Expedited Regulatory Reporting timeline, with a final submission deadline of 27-OCT-2026
